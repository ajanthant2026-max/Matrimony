Micro-interaction Specification
↔
Card swipe gesture
Drag triggers rotate + translate. Right tints gold (connect), left tints cool grey (pass). Release threshold at 40% — springs back with cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) if under threshold.
✦
Interest chip selection
Scale spring (1 → 1.04 → 1.02) + check mark fade-in. 4ms haptic pulse on mobile. Deselect reverses with 150ms ease-out.
↑
Feed scroll reveal
Cards enter with translateY(24px) → 0 + opacity 0→1 staggered 80ms apart. Intersection Observer triggers — only animates cards entering viewport.
❤
Like / heart tap
Heart icon pulses to 1.4× scale with crimson fill, then settles to 1.1× with a brief gold particle burst (3 dots, CSS keyframes). Button stays filled to confirm state.
→
Onboarding step transition
Full-screen slide-left (translateX 0→−100%) on Continue, step dot animates simultaneously. Gold CTA pulses once on landing to draw attention.
